## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions: Adding Services to Google Business Profile

1. Go to **[Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com)** and sign in.
2. Select your business: **Friends Graphics**.
3. Click on the **Edit Profile** button > select **Services**.
4. Check your **Primary Category** (`Commercial Printer`) and add your **Secondary Categories** (`Print Shop`, `Sign Shop`, `Digital Printing Service`, `Offset Printing Service`, `Banner Store`).
5. Under each category, click **+ Add more services** > select or type the exact **Service Name**.
6. Click into the newly created service > Click **Add description**.
7. Copy and paste the exact **Description** text block from this guide (every description is pre-checked to be under the 300-character limit).
8. Click **Save**.
